# Refund Policy

Customers on annual plans can request a prorated refund within 30 days of renewal. Refunds
after 30 days are not offered, but unused seats can be credited against the next invoice.

Monthly plans are not refunded; cancelling stops the next charge.

Refund requests go to the account manager, who needs approval from finance for any amount
over $5,000.
